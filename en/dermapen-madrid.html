Dermapen is a collagen induction microneedling treatment that lasts 1 hour. It uses a micro-needle device to stimulate the skin's natural regeneration, improving acne scarring, fine lines, blemishes, enlarged pores and sagging. At FAOR Beauty Skin it costs €80 and is suitable for both women and men.

The treatment

How it works

01

Microneedling

Controlled micro-perforations in the skin that trigger cell renewal.

02

Active ingredient penetration

Vitamins, hyaluronic acid or exosomes are applied to enhance results.

03

Overall rejuvenation

Collagen and elastin regenerate, improving texture, radiance and firmness.

Who it's for

When Dermapen helps

  • Acne scarring and uneven skin texture.
  • Enlarged or clogged pores.
  • Fine lines and early signs of sagging.
  • Blemishes or uneven skin tone.
  • Men looking to improve texture and firmness with no makeup routine.
  • Combinable with Hydrafacial, Deep Cleansing, Exosomes or DNA Plus.
Before booking

What you should know

Recommendations

  • Skin may show redness or slight flaking in the 24-48h afterwards.
  • Avoid makeup and sun exposure for at least 48h afterwards.
  • Always use sunscreen after the session.
  • A multi-session protocol is recommended for optimal results.
  • Avoid saunas, pools and intense exercise for 48h afterwards.

Restrictions

  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding.
  • Coagulation disorders or use of blood thinners.
  • Infections, herpes, eczema or active acne in the area.
  • Recent scarring, burns or severe irritation.
  • Recent aggressive dermatological treatments (laser, deep peels).
