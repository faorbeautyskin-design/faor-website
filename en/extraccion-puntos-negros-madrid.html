Who it's for

When an extraction makes sense

  • Blackheads on the nose, chin or forehead that your daily routine can't shift.
  • Clogged pores, oily or shiny skin.
  • Men looking for an effective, no-fuss facial treatment.
  • Skin maintenance every 4-6 weeks.
  • Before an event, for cleaner, more radiant skin.
  • A first step before treatments such as Dermapen or Hollywood Peel.
Before you book

What you should know

Recommendations

  • Don't come with heavy makeup on the day of your appointment.
  • Avoid intense sun exposure or UVA rays in the 48h before.
  • Don't use products with acids or retinol in the 72h before.
  • If you're on a dermatological treatment (such as Roaccutane), check with us before booking.
  • After the session, avoid makeup, direct sun, pools and saunas for 24-48h.

Restrictions

  • Active skin infections (herpes, eczema, dermatitis, severe inflammatory acne).
  • Recent sunburn.
  • Open wounds or previous aggressive treatments (laser, strong chemical peel, micropigmentation).
  • Use of oral retinoids (Roaccutane) without medical approval.
FAQ

Your questions, answered

Does blackhead extraction hurt?

You may feel slight discomfort during extraction, but it is not a painful treatment. Ozone steaming preps the pores and the final soothing mask helps reduce any sensitivity.

How much does blackhead extraction cost at FAOR Beauty Skin?

Extraction is included in the Deep Facial Cleansing (1 h, €75). For a more intensive cleanse, the Hydrodermabrasion Facial + Extraction costs €140 (1 h 30 min).

What is the difference between the Deep Facial Cleansing and the Hydrodermabrasion Facial + Extraction?

The Deep Facial Cleansing is the essential option, with manual extraction. The Hydrodermabrasion Facial + Extraction adds painless suction extraction and intense hydration with serums, ideal for skin with many impurities or for a more complete result.

Can I remove blackheads at home?

It isn't recommended. Squeezing with your fingers tends to irritate the skin, can push part of the impurity deeper and leave marks. In our treatment room the skin is prepped first, extraction is done with professional technique and the skin is soothed at the end.

How often should I get an extraction?

Every 4-6 weeks is recommended to keep pores clear, although it depends on each person's skin type.

Will my skin be red afterwards?

Mild, temporary redness may appear after extraction. We recommend avoiding makeup, direct sun, saunas and pools for 24-48h.

Is this treatment suitable for men?

Yes. Deep facial cleansing is one of the treatments most requested by men at our center, especially for clogged pores, excess oil or blackheads.
