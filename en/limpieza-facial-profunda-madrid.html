Deep facial cleansing is a 1-hour treatment that restores the skin's purity and balance through exfoliation, ozone steaming, manual extraction of impurities and a soothing mask. At FAOR Beauty Skin it costs €75 and is suitable for both women and men with oily skin, clogged pores or blackheads.

The treatment

What the session includes

01

Exfoliation

Removes dead cells and preps the skin for the rest of the protocol.

02

Ozone steaming

Opens the pores and eases the extraction of impurities.

03

Extraction

Manual removal of blackheads and excess oil.

04

Soothing mask

Hydrates, balances and reduces redness to close the session.

Who it's for

When this cleansing helps

  • Skin with clogged pores, blackheads or uneven texture.
  • Men looking for a simple facial treatment, no makeup or extra steps.
  • Dull or oily skin that needs to breathe.
  • Before an event, for fresher, brighter-looking skin.
  • Regular skin maintenance, every 4-6 weeks.
  • Recommended first step before more specific treatments (Dermapen, Hollywood Peel).
Before booking

What you should know

Recommendations

  • Don't come with heavy makeup on the day of your appointment.
  • Avoid intense sun exposure or UVA rays in the 48h before.
  • Don't use products with acids or retinol in the 72h before.
  • If you're on a dermatological treatment (such as Roaccutane), check with us before booking.
  • After the session, avoid direct sun, pools and saunas for 48h.

Restrictions

  • Active skin infections (herpes, eczema, dermatitis, severe inflammatory acne).
  • Recent sunburn.
  • Open wounds or previous aggressive treatments (laser, strong chemical peel, micropigmentation).
  • Use of oral retinoids (Roaccutane) without medical approval.

Frequently asked questions

Your questions, answered

How long does a deep facial cleansing take?

The session lasts about 1 hour and includes exfoliation, ozone steaming, extraction of impurities and a soothing mask.

How much does deep facial cleansing cost at FAOR Beauty Skin?

The price is €75 per session at our center in the Salamanca neighborhood, Madrid.

Is deep facial cleansing painful?

You may feel slight discomfort during the extraction of blackheads and impurities, but it's not a painful treatment. The ozone steaming and soothing mask help reduce any sensitivity.

Does deep facial cleansing work for men?

Yes. It's one of the most requested treatments by men at our center, especially for skin with clogged pores, excess oil or blackheads, with no makeup or extra steps needed.

How often should I get a deep facial cleansing?

It's recommended every 4-6 weeks to keep pores clear and skin balanced, though it depends on each person's skin type.

What care should I take before and after the session?

Before: avoid intense sun exposure 48h before and products with acids or retinol in the 72h before. After: avoid direct sun, pools and saunas for 48h to let the skin recover properly.
